TerraBot: an autonomous bot that plays Terraria by pressing the same controls a player would.

Milestone 1: toggle the bot, pick a target tile, and watch it path there by walking, jumping and dropping.
Keybinds (rebind under Controls > Mod Controls): toggle bot, set target to tile under cursor.
Chat: /bot goto <x> <y>, /bot stop, /bot dump, /bot trace, /bot status.
